# Design System: Mimo

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Stay with the conversation"**

Mimo gives accepted understanding a vivid, stable place to read. Saturated violet anchors the family; pale periwinkle or midnight surfaces keep supporting tasks legible. The original mint jelly identity adds a friendly, tactile presence through transparent artwork, expressive poses, and bounded squash and stretch.

Native system type and direct controls suit sustained listening and reading in Chrome. Expression belongs to Mimo and short interaction responses. Accepted text stays still while the companion acknowledges a real change. The Daylight and Midnight world continues from the preceding frontend; the Mimo 0.13.0 source candidate retains understanding and the active contribution on the same violet reading ground, with Mimo on its upper seam and unboxed recording controls directly below the character on Live memo. Stop now leads to a separate Meeting notes reader on that field, with factual bullets first and file status/actions below. The candidate remains separate from installed 0.12.1; current evidence and limits are in `artifacts/end-meeting-2026-11-02/IMPLEMENTATION.md`.

**Key Characteristics:**

- A constant violet reading field for understanding and the active contribution in Daylight and Midnight.
- One reading space: understanding is home; an additional thought replaces it while being read.
- A transparent mint jelly identity with eight shared production poses and a canonical fallback.
- System sans, labeled navigation, and reachable recording controls.
- Bounded event-driven motion with reduced-motion and visibility safeguards.

## Colors

### Primary

Live violet owns accepted understanding and the active contribution in one assistant reading surface, the assistant idle field, the popup listening stage, and Settings headings. Its inverse ink remains white in both themes. Muted inverse text is specific to each surface; keep the source's semantic assignment. Darker hero violet supports readiness and Back controls. The popup's pale action surface is a distinct token.

### Secondary

Daylight uses a deep periwinkle action accent; Midnight uses pale periwinkle. These carry selected navigation, links, focus, feedback selection, and source controls. Mimo's mint and lavender shading belong to the generated artwork, rather than a flat CSS action fill.

### Neutral

Daylight combines a pale periwinkle page, white task panels, blue-black text, and slate secondary text. Midnight combines an ink page, lighter ink task panels, pale text, and lavender-slate secondary text. Live reading uses inverse text on violet in both themes; utility views retain the theme shell. Fine lines separate utility, response, and history content. The unboxed live clock uses muted inverse ink; the icon-only Stop uses white on the violet reading ground.

Success, warning, and error/danger colors remain surface-specific. Prefixed frontmatter tokens preserve the implemented assistant, Settings, and popup pairs; they are not interchangeable global semantic values. Written labels and source controls carry meaning alongside color.

## Typography

All surfaces use the native system sans stack in the frontmatter. Display type belongs to the idle assistant greeting; Settings uses headline type. Accepted narrative uses memo type; a focused contribution uses thought-title and thought-body. Popup intro, source heading, and wordmark retain separate scales. No external font is required.

Understanding and contribution prose share an 18px/1.65 reading scale and a maximum measure of 68ch. Their headings share 20px/600/1.4 with −0.025em tracking. At width ≤300px, body becomes 17px/1.55 and headings 18px; at ≤240px, body is 16px/1.6 and headings 17px. Settings headings become 24px at 280px. The three assistant footer labels retain 11px/1.25 in one row, including native 200% zoom. The timer beneath Mimo uses 12px type and tabular numerals. Preserve complete accepted text; long or extreme cases may scroll.

**The Stable Reading Rule.** Preserve accepted text, reading position, and keyboard focus during updates to the current reading view. Put the visible acknowledgement on Mimo.

## Layout

One flat violet surface holds understanding, a focused contribution, or the post-meeting reader for the selected ended session. On Live memo, Mimo perches on the upper seam; an unboxed row immediately beneath the visible mascot body contains actual elapsed time and an independent icon-only Stop. Reserve enough reading inset for both controls. The former header, lower character stage, nested thought panel, and separate session capsule are superseded.

Only empty sprite space is removed from the target height. Stop remains a disjoint 44×44px target with an accessible action name at every width. Its hover fill and keyboard-focus outline use a 26px circle inside that target. The cue is visually hidden with its existing live announcement retained. The 18px round contribution-availability marker sits near the tuft at top 4px/right 6px and remains distinct from the face.

The welcome card keeps positive outer space and complete rounded corners; Settings belongs inside that card. Start listening keeps horizontal text and icon, opaque interaction states, and a wrapping source title below it. Idle embedded Settings places its overview return inside the hero, immediately before Settings, and has no footer or parent navigation strip. Live and retained reading use one three-column footer row: Live memo, Transcript, Settings. All three keep their labels, icons, and selected states, with 52px minimum targets and 11px labels. Settings is an idempotent destination; normal Transcript does not add a Current understanding return action. Source-opened Transcript keeps its contextual return. Session history is a secondary row inside the Settings overview when available. The popup is 320px wide. Its violet stage has 12px side margins, a 360px idle minimum height, and 276px during recording/stopping/saving. The greeting target is 136px square, becoming 96px during capture. Start listening is a separate full-width action. The footer is at least 62px high.

Standalone Settings uses a 520px maximum main area and 28px side gutters, reducing to 20px at 400px. Embedded Settings follows its host width and footer. Embedded and standalone Settings keep their normal portrait because the Live companion is hidden on Settings. The existing trusted host-visibility message remains the boundary for portrait suppression. Primary rows are at least 66px high; secondary rows are 58px. At 280px leading row icons hide and labels become 12px. Settings overview and detail wrappers are named inline-size containers (`mimo-settings`). At container width ≤240px, the portrait/name enters its own normal-flow row separate from heading/readiness content; the greeting target is 44px and readiness wraps at word boundaries. The container threshold responds to actual available width, including CSS zoom, rather than only viewport width. Wide standalone overview adds top spacing from 640px.

## Elevation & Depth

Tonal layers and fine borders define the operating shell. Understanding and the active contribution share one flat reading ground. Their internal containers are transparent, borderless, and square inside the outer rounded slab. The older separate-panel and bubble-shadow rules remain in superseded CSS and are not current component guidance.

Inputs use a stronger control border than list dividers. Focus normally uses a 2px outline with a 3px offset, white inside violet regions. Popup targets use a 4px offset; the upper-seam companion uses a white 2px outline with a 3px offset plus a dark 5px surrounding ring so focus remains visible against both shell and violet. The contribution region retains an inset outline. Selected navigation and feedback retain explicit accessible state.

## Shapes

Soft rectangles join the family: the shared reading slab, Settings heading/list, and popup-stage corners use the reading-field scale. The live understanding and contribution containers add no inner corners or border. Inputs and ordinary controls use the control scale; navigation and popup Start use the navigation scale. Useful and Skip use the response scale. The source retains separate smaller feedback, kind, and marker shapes for supporting content.

Mimo's silhouette comes from transparent production rasters. The upper-seam character uses the character-seam geometry; the idle greeting retains character-target geometry. Popup and Settings retain their own transparent targets. Keep their reading footprints stable during pose changes.

## Components

### Buttons

Settings primary actions are content-sized periwinkle controls with inverse ink and a 44px minimum height. Hover and active use explicit action variants. Secondary actions use the theme tint and normal ink. Text actions stay quiet until hover. Sources inside understanding are inverse controls with a fine live-control border and a 44px minimum height. Useful is white with dark violet ink; Skip is dark violet with white ink, a 44px minimum width and height, and a deeper hover fill. Disabled controls retain a truthful label.

Assistant and popup greetings are separate from Start listening. The assistant idle greeting is 180px square before height-based compaction; its Start action is a white content-sized horizontal button with a 48px minimum height and explicit opaque hover/active fills. The popup Start action has a 46px minimum height, lifts 2px on hover, and scales to 0.97 on press. Greeting Mimo does not start recording.

### Inputs / Fields

Native input, select, and textarea controls use the panel surface, control border, control corners, and 12px by 14px padding. Minimum height is 46px; objective textareas are at least 128px. Keep visible labels and relevant transfer/key disclosure. The key eye toggle has a 44px target. Inline error color and aria-invalid identify invalid input; recoverable failures retain drafts.

### Navigation

Live memo, Transcript, and Settings retain visible labels beneath stroke SVG icons in one row. Session history is a secondary Settings overview row when the host has content; History Back returns focus to that row and preserves the underlying Live or Transcript route. A saved reader has Back to start. Selected navigation uses accent, tint, and aria-pressed. The Settings label and icon remain Settings when selected, and repeated activation stays on that route. The Live companion, duration, and icon-only Stop are limited to Live memo; use that tab to reach Stop. Ordinary Transcript omits a Current understanding return action. Sources open matching timestamped transcript rows; Back restores the exact contribution and initiating control when still available. Popup and standalone Settings footer actions retain accessible names and visible labels.

**The One Return per Level Rule.** In Settings, show the return for the current level inside its content. Idle embedded overview has one icon-only Back to Mimo immediately before Settings; details keep only their existing Back to Settings. Active Settings uses the stable footer tabs, and standalone Settings has no host-return or history affordance.

The overview return is a separate 44px square target with a 20px stroke SVG, 10px padding, control-scale corners, transparent rest state, darker violet hover fill, and a white focus outline. Its heading group has an 8px gap, reducing to 4px at Settings container width ≤240px. At that container width, a visible overview return pairs with a 24px Settings title; this is component geometry, not a new general type scale. Host-state updates change available host actions without resetting detail screens or drafts. History return targets the child row rather than generic iframe autofocus.

### Cards / Containers

Current understanding and the active contribution share the stable violet field. Understanding retains its label, accepted narrative, update time, and Sources. Accepted state survives processing, rejected proposals, and recoverable errors. Automatic work and queued retry keep the accepted reading state without a manual Try again action; a paused failure remains actionable. Transcript quality and delay notices stay with Transcript.

Settings uses bordered grouped lists and violet detail headings. Contribution history uses line-separated entries, loads 25 at a time, and identifies historical status as past status. Saved understanding and history remain inspectable after Stop.

### Meeting notes and export

The post-meeting reader reuses the inverse violet field and existing Mimo artwork. Factual bullets lead without forced category headings. A quiet status line describes actual transcription/generation, failure, or empty content; a divider separates Audio, Transcript and Meeting notes save states and the applicable Choose folder, Retry meeting notes, Retry saving, New meeting and Discard controls. Do not claim saved files or available minutes from an in-progress or failed state.

The heading is 24px/1.25 at weight 650, reducing to 22px at ≤240px. Bullets are 17px/1.65 and reduce to 16px at ≤280px. The status is 14px/1.6, save summary/actions 13px, and per-file metadata 12px. Ended top inset is 76px, 64px at ≤280px and 52px at ≤240px; gutters are 24px, reducing to 16px at ≤280px. Bullets use the existing mint identity as marker color. Action targets are at least 44px; Sources retains the authored 36px target. The full reader scrolls inside its field and preserves stable footer destinations.

Save-only updates retain the bullet DOM and its Sources control. Source navigation uses the selected job's own transcript and restores initiating focus on Back. Stop-owned focus moves into the visible Meeting notes heading after capture closes. Other unsaved meetings is a secondary disclosure; selecting one cannot take over a new recording. Detailed source and evidence boundaries are in `.impeccable/surfaces/end-meeting.md` and `docs/END-MEETING.md`. Native OS folder selection remains separate from renderer, OPFS writer and keyboard-focus proof.

### Chips / Feedback

Focused contributions show their actual title first, followed by body, timestamp, checked status, meaningful kind/epistemic markers, sources, and response controls. Titleless contributions use “A thought to consider.” Useful and Skip are separate 44px controls. More discloses Not helpful and Dismiss. Failed feedback stays on the contribution with a retryable message. Previous-topic text is muted; answered status uses success and corrected status uses warning. Conversation sources provide context and do not verify general knowledge.

### Mimo and the focused contribution

**The Contribution Rule.** A contribution temporarily owns the reading space. Useful, Skip, Mimo, or Escape returns to the latest understanding.

The shared production sheet is `public/mimo/mimo-expressions.png`, a 1774×887 transparent RGBA PNG with four columns and two rows: idle, listening, thinking, insight, appreciation, curious, rest, reassure. It retains the canonical mint identity at `public/memo/memo-slime.png` (1254×1254 RGBA), which remains the failure fallback and manifest icon source. Both were generated with built-in ImageGen; exact prompts are embedded under `impeccable:prompt` and retained in `docs/design-assets/`. The sheet is used across assistant, popup, and Settings. Preserve provenance and transparent contours. Developer direction contracts stay outside browser assets.

A genuinely new accepted contribution enters once when understanding is visible; if that hides the focused reading region, focus moves to the visible reading region. Restored/repeated snapshots stay quiet. Newer thoughts queue behind the displayed thought. Clicking Mimo can revisit an available thought. Skip, Mimo, and Escape change local presentation; Dismiss archives explicitly through feedback. The former Close toolbar is hidden in the upper-edge surface. Useful waits for successful storage, then returns with appreciation, guarded by session, contribution, and interaction generation. Source/Back restores the pinned reading view. Stop suppresses automatic contribution entry and opens the separate complete-transcript meeting-notes flow. Ended Mimo cannot reopen old live contributions. Preparation, ready, empty and failed results need truthful distinct cues; source inspection and save-only progress preserve the reading context.

**The Real Change Rule.** React to actual accepted state, recording state, source/history navigation, recovery, or direct character interaction. Repeated snapshots stay quiet.

Shared reactions use an 800ms duration, except insight/Useful at 1100ms, with `cubic-bezier(.22,.8,.3,1)` and a center 78% transform origin. Temporary expressions settle after 1700ms when moving and 1000ms without movement. Listening breath is bounded to three 4-second cycles. Pointer look is capped at 3px horizontally, 2px vertically, and 3 degrees; press applies a local squash. Hidden documents cancel transient animation and reset pointer state. Reduced motion retains pose/state and operability while skipping motion. Settings detail reveal is 220ms; popup controls use 160ms transitions. No movement types text, simulates audio, or claims progress.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** use the shared Daylight and Midnight palette across popup, assistant, and Settings.
- **Do** keep the shared violet reading field and inverse text consistent across both themes.
- **Do** use the shared Mimo pose sheet with its canonical fallback and preserve transparent contours and provenance.
- **Do** preserve independent accepted state, source return, feedback, reading position, and keyboard focus.
- **Do** keep the unboxed real duration and separate icon-only Stop directly beneath Mimo on Live memo, with a 44px Stop target and stable labeled tabs.
- **Do** keep core actions usable with reduced motion or failed artwork.

### Don't:

- **Don't** revive the retired Listen wordmark, rounded-square mascot substitute, activity strip, or update banners on the live reading surface.
- **Don't** stack a contribution beneath understanding or overlay it as a speech bubble in the current live surface.
