## Behavioral boundaries

- Skip, Mimo and Escape are local presentation actions; they do not submit negative feedback or archive a contribution.
- Useful returns only after successful feedback storage and only for the same session, displayed contribution and interaction generation. Failed feedback stays actionable.
- Restored/closed sessions and repeated state do not automatically reopen a contribution. A newer contribution queues during an existing reading view or unrelated navigation.
- Stop suppresses automatic entry and returns to accepted understanding. No recap/MOM is created.
- Complete accepted text is rendered directly. Motion does not type out fabricated generation, reveal model reasoning, simulate audio levels, or move reading text.
- Motion pauses when hidden and respects reduced-motion preferences. Core navigation and feedback work without animation or new artwork.
- Frontend validation uses offline fixtures and actual-extension mocks. The historical 800 ledger and the later authorized 204/240 live run are preserved; no new ASR/LLM evaluation is authorized by this design task.
