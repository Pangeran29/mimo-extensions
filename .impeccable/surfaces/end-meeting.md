# End meeting

Mode: Operate. Existing-world extension of the approved Mimo interface. The 2026-11-02 user direction supersedes the old rule that Stop never prepares minutes. No new visual identity, fonts, or raster assets.

Thesis: Stop the recording boundary immediately, then give the completed conversation a factual reading surface and a recoverable three-file save.

The post-meeting reader owns the same violet field as live understanding. Mimo retains the upper-seam identity and stable footer destinations; elapsed time and Stop disappear once capture ends. The reader starts with Meeting notes, actual-stage copy, then plain factual bullets. Per the user's 0.13.3 direction, Sources controls no longer appear beneath minutes bullets; the Transcript navigation retains access to the selected result's stored transcript. A fine divider introduces the chosen folder, Audio/Transcript/Meeting notes save states, and only applicable actions. Other unsaved meetings remains a secondary disclosure with a brief explanation of local retained sessions. No live thought is reopened from ended Mimo.

The heading is 24px/1.25 at weight 650, reducing to 22px at ≤240px. Bullet text is 17px/1.65, reducing to 16px at ≤280px. Status is 14px/1.6; save summary/actions are 13px, and file metadata 12px. Mint bullet markers, inverse white ink and muted periwinkle reuse the incumbent palette. Ended top inset is 76px, 64px at ≤280px and 52px at ≤240px. Reading gutters are 24px or 16px at ≤280px. Actions retain at least 44px targets; authored Sources remains 36px. Content can scroll inside the field rather than pushing footer navigation away.

The native picker begins in the original click after Stop has been dispatched. Cancellation retains results and Choose folder. Capture and playback do not wait for a folder, final ASR, model generation or disk writes. Meeting notes wait for a complete ordered transcript, and live publication is stopped independently. Audio, transcript and minutes save separately; the interface cannot claim all files saved while any remain pending/failed.

Save-only updates preserve the bullet DOM and keyboard focus. Transcript uses the selected result's own snapshot. A Stop-owned transition focuses the visible Meeting notes heading, then Tab reaches a visible action. Old result updates cannot replace a new recording or steal an explicitly selected older reader. New meeting hides the ended reader without discarding unfinished work. Discard uses an inline group on the existing reading surface, with meeting identity, explicit consequences, Keep meeting as initial focus, and a separate Discard meeting action. It has no native dialog dependency, no modal overlay, and no automatic confirmation. Leaving the result cancels it. Files already saved to a folder remain untouched.

Mimo's preparing, ready, empty and failed cues must match the actual result. The initial finish review found a ready cue in failed/empty states; that correction and its follow-up evidence must be read separately from the base screenshots. Character motion remains bounded and does not certify progress, factual accuracy or export success.
