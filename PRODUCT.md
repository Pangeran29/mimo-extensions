## Product Purpose

## Users and Operating Context

People listening to meetings or other audio from a Chrome website tab. The assistant appears in a narrow docked side panel or a wider extension window. A 320px popup starts capture and opens the live memo; Settings works embedded in the assistant or in a standalone options/first-install tab. The listener must be able to follow audio, read accepted state, inspect evidence, and stop recording without losing their place.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Microphone and one Chrome tab are captured; other tabs and desktop apps are not. The existing three encoders remain temporary capture storage. New meetings export one combined WebM plus timestamped transcript and bullet minutes to a unique chosen session folder; old retained recordings keep legacy separate-audio recovery.
- Transcription uses service-managed DeepInfra Qwen3-ASR 0.6B through a loopback development relay. No customer transcription key, local Whisper download, or WebGPU recognition is required. Packaged VAD remains. Cloud failures retain speech windows locally and retry while recording continues, including after Stop; there is no local-model fallback. Durable pending windows recover after offscreen restart or Chrome startup; new sessions wait for recovery. Public deployment and per-user usage accounting remain deferred. The user accepted the current ASR quality and cloud latency on 2026-10-25.
- AI is required for every session, as confirmed by the user on 2026-10-17. AI connection offers HPOP (Claude Sonnet 5.5) or managed DeepInfra (`deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017`). HPOP remains the default and retains its saved key/permission. Managed DeepInfra has no customer key field. Provider changes occur between meetings; automatic and deeper analysis use the selected provider without fallback. Transcript, objective, and context leave the device for that provider; charges may apply.
- Queued contiguous audio windows merge up to 16 seconds when the serial ASR worker becomes available, without an extra collection timer. The eight-second individual-window build option remains available for rollback. The frontend revamp does not change ASR or provider behavior.
- Keys and preferences persist in Chrome local storage restricted to trusted extension contexts across restarts and reloads; keys are not synced. Remove key deletes local and legacy session copies and blocks new AI-required sessions. Saving configuration is not proof of remote credential validity. The objective remains an optional persisted default for future sessions.
- V2 stores the accepted memo, independent active contribution, checked relevance status, sources, history, feedback, and working memory. Current understanding is one or two sentences, capped at 600 characters. Additional contribution targets 30–90 words with a 1,200-character body cap. A proposal/reviewer chain accepts public components independently. Processing, empty/rejected results, and failures retain the last accepted understanding. Source-linked memory and output checks are bounded; they are not a guarantee of semantic accuracy.
- Inherited from the 0.12.2 live-recovery candidate, a validated proposal commits source-linked memory and its transcript cursor once. If review transport or validation fails, pending publication retries only review without waiting for new speech or repeating that memory commit. Consecutive failed rounds pause after three attempts; manual resume respects retained provider backoff. A valid rejection is a completed decision. New transcript, feedback, Stop, disabled state, restore, and session changes fence or cancel obsolete work. Pending review counts as busy at replay EOF, subject to the existing 60-second drain limit. Pending publication is ephemeral and does not recover across reload/restore.
- Inherited live-request deadlines are shared across controller and transport: proposal 20 seconds, v1 review 10 seconds, v2 review 15 seconds; legacy/deeper analysis remains 30 seconds. The v2 extension is an empirical experiment based on two historical ten-second partial JSON cutoffs, not proof of a completed valid response or provider health. Relay 429/503 backoff accepts delta-seconds or HTTP-date and is exposed to the permitted origin; its explicit 29-second pre-header timeout is 504, while HTTP 408/504 classify as timeout. Live model/provider choices, prompts, ASR, protocols and storage schemas remain unchanged; the end-meeting protocol and job store are separate additions.
- Contributions persist until replacement or Dismiss. The current source shows one contribution in the reading space in place of understanding. Useful waits for saved feedback, then returns to the latest understanding with appreciation; Skip, Mimo, and Escape return locally without negative feedback or archival. The previous Close toolbar is hidden in the upper-edge surface. New thoughts queue while the displayed thought or another task is being read. Checked statuses are relevant, previous topic, answered, or corrected, with status evidence where available. Dismiss clears only the contribution. Earlier entries and feedback remain in Session history under Settings, loaded 25 at a time; historical status describes the last shown state. Latest-session understanding and history remain accessible after Stop. Legacy notes are explicitly separated as notes from a previous version.
- Sources open the timestamped conversation and return to the originating reading view. Interpretation, general knowledge, and suggestions are labeled separately from participant statements. Conversation sources provide context and do not verify general knowledge. Analyze deeper uses current understanding and relevant context; it is not a MOM action.
- Stop aborts automatic/deeper publication and saves accepted live state. The independent end-meeting controller waits for complete final ASR, generates and reviews factual minutes, and saves eligible files separately. Folder cancellation and generation/write failures retain local work. Recovery does not reactivate live output. Empty transcripts produce an explicit empty result without a model call. AI persistence or processing failure must not stop recording/ASR.
- End-meeting jobs checkpoint transcript identity, extraction coverage and generation/review progress. Source IDs and exact quotes must match; these checks do not certify semantic truth, ASR wording or dominant-language quality. Production keeps the latest fully saved completed reader plus all unfinished local jobs, not a cloud archive. Audio is released after all three files save; interrupted processing requires explicit retry, and opening a restored reader performs no automatic model request or file write.
- Implementation is TypeScript, native HTML/CSS, esbuild, and Manifest V3. Preserve extension API boundaries and existing safeguards. The 0.13.0 candidate adds no manifest permissions and preserves extension identity, credentials, ASR and live provider/model choices. It adds separate end-meeting prompts/protocols and a job/handle store, while retaining existing live-v2 behavior and its bounded recovery. No model is certified as a recommended default; do not invent usefulness, latency, performance, or pricing claims.

## Product Principles

- Explain prerequisites before starting and keep recoverable failures actionable.
- Distinguish saved configuration, verified connectivity, and operational readiness.
- Preserve input, accepted understanding, reading position, and focus through recoverable changes.
- Keep local storage and external audio/text transfer boundaries understandable.
- Judge understanding by factual clarity and current focus; judge contributions by supported, timely usefulness. Allow either output to wait independently.
- Keep live assistance and full-transcript end-of-meeting generation independent.
