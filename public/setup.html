How Mimo joins in

Your current understanding follows the conversation. When Mimo has a useful contribution, it gets its own reading space.

Useful saves your feedback and returns to the understanding. Skip, Close, or Escape returns without a rating. You can ask Mimo to reopen an available contribution.

What do Sources and labels mean?

Sources open the transcript passages behind an output. Interpretation, general knowledge, and suggestions are labeled separately from what participants said. Conversation sources provide context; they do not verify outside knowledge.

Conversation diagnostics

Includes transcript passages, instructions, model responses and saved notes. A report downloads after you stop and final processing finishes. Reports stay on this device; keys and audio are excluded. The latest four reports are retained locally, up to 16 MiB of captured events per meeting. Review contents before sharing.

Diagnose a problem

Download the latest failure details and app version. The report contains no key, transcript, meeting notes or audio. No recording or test request is started. 


          
What’s recorded

          

            Mimo captures your microphone and one Chrome tab. Other tabs and desktop apps are not
            captured. Let participants know when recording; use Stop & Save to finish.
          

          
What stays local

          

            Audio recordings and transcript copies stay on this device. Pending speech windows are
            retained locally until transcription succeeds.
          

          
What goes to the cloud

          

            Speech windows go through our transcription service to DeepInfra. Transcript text, your
            objective, and meeting context go to your selected notes provider: HPOP and its model
            provider, or DeepInfra. API charges may apply. Model speed and usefulness are
            experimental.
          

          
Your AI connection key

          

            Your key stays in local storage on this device until you remove it or uninstall the
            extension. It is not synced to other devices. Saving a connection checks configuration,
            not remote key validity.
          

          
Before listening

          

            Microphone access and AI configuration are required. Cloud transcription connects
            automatically. Open a website and press Start listening in the Mimo popup.
